Owen McAteer is an Irish new media artist, based in Madrid. He creates living artworks using code and mathematical systems that manifest as kinetic flipdot displays and interactive installations of colour & light.

His art practice explores the human connection with the hidden world around us and our desire to understand it, and in doing so, uncover the beauty within the unpredictable.
