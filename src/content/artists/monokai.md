Monokai is the creative studio of Wimer Hazenberg. He works at the intersection of code and design.

In 2006 he created a [color scheme for developers](https://monokai.pro) — also called Monokai — which is being used around the world.

He creates algorithmic artworks made entirely with code, where a carefully calibrated computational space generates results that are both unexpected and within the intended constraints.
