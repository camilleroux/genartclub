Andrea Belloni is a generative artist whose work emerges from the intersection
of his scientific background and his passion for computer graphics. Holding a
Physics degree from La Sapienza University in Rome, where he developed a
fascination for complex systems, chaos theory, and fractals, Andrea spent
several years working in robotics, control systems, and machine learning. His
artistic practice is defined by a unique constraint: every piece is the product
of custom code he writes himself, deliberately eschewing traditional digital
art tools or post-processing software.
